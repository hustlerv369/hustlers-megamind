How it works

Four hooks. Zero invocation.

Megamind installs four lifecycle hooks into ~/.claude/settings.json. After that there's nothing to call — they fire automatically on every session, on every machine you sync your config to.

Hook
What it does
Cost
SessionStart
Injects a line-clipped memory map + the newest clean session note + a lean directive.

01 Memory

Remembers your project across sessions. Smart recall scores by term-frequency, coverage, title & freshness — and skips transcript-dump noise entirely.

02 Context discipline

Knows where tokens really go and gives you the levers: opus[1m]→opus, xhigh→high, per-project MCP, subagents for big reads.

03 Lazy skills

Skills you haven't used in 30 days stop loading their descriptions. Re-enable any in one command. Only megamind is always protected.
